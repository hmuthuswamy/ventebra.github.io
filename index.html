Engineering principles

Make the reliable path the easy path.

  • Operable by designSystems should be understandable under normal conditions and under pressure.
  • Automation with intentAutomation should reduce toil while preserving review, control, and recovery paths.
  • Change with confidenceDelivery systems should make risk visible before production does.
  • Maintainable foundationsPlatforms should make sound defaults easier to adopt and evolve.
